Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Who we are

AppointEez ("we", "us") runs appointeezleads.com. AppointEez is a trading name of Rory Fox, 66 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NA. Contact: hello@appointeezleads.com.

We help home service contractors reactivate their own old leads and past quotes with text messages and an AI assistant that books appointments.

Information we collect

  • When you book a call or contact us: your name, email, phone number, business name and anything you tell us. Bookings are handled by our scheduling provider (LeadConnector).
  • When a contractor uses our service: we process the contractor's list of past enquiries and customers (such as name, phone number and job details) on the contractor's behalf, and the replies those people send.
  • Technical information: standard details such as browser type, device and IP address, collected by our hosting provider.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, hold calls, and follow up by call, text or email about your enquiry.
  • To run the service a contractor has asked for: sending text messages, replying to answers and booking appointments into that contractor's calendar.
  • To keep this site and our service working and secure.

We do not sell personal information.

Text messages and consent

We only message past enquiries and customers who agreed to be contacted by text. Every message includes an opt-out, and anyone who opts out is removed straight away. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and text messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that help us run this site and our service (for example hosting, scheduling, texting and email), and only as needed for that work. Information about a contractor's customers is shared with that contractor. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires. When a contractor stops using our service, we stop processing their customers' details.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@appointeezleads.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
